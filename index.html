Which platform for the site?

Squarespace, Framer, or a custom stack. All three can look good — the differences are design limits, who can edit what, and how stable the brand stays after launch. Ordering stays on ChowNow + Toast either way.

Suggested direction
Custom stack

Best if you want the Odd+Even look locked in and a content editor built for Molly’s (hours, FAQ, catering, seasonal pages) without staff being able to break the design. Framer is the main alternative if launch speed matters more than a tailored editor. Squarespace is the weakest brand fit.
